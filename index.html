About

I am Tanya Kaushal, an international financial journalist with experience in data analysis and visualization. My experience spans UK, US and India, including coverage on Wall Street, economy, labor and private equity.

Most recently, I was based in London working as a reporter at the trade publication, The Drawdown. I wrote cover features for the monhtly magazine, hosted podcasts, moderated industry panels and wrote several long-form analytical pieces. In the US, I was a data reporter at Yahoo Finance creating graphics for all beats in the newsroom and writing data-led features.

I was named runner-up in the 2025 Journalist of the Year: Investor Services / Asset Servicing category at The State Street UK Institutional Press Awards for my article on consolidation in the fund administration market.

My beginnings in journalism began as a teen growing up in India writing for the school paper and later interning at the national daily, The Times of India.

Days when I am not analyzing financial numbers, you can find me at a matcha cafe or bookstore.
