About

I am an international financial journalist with expertise in data analysis and visualization. My experience spans UK, US and India, covering Wall Street, economy, labor, and private equity.

In London, I reported on European private markets via long-form features, magazine cover stories, newsletters, and podcasts. I worked with Yahoo Finance in New York as a data reporter creating graphics for nearly all beats in the newsroom and producing data-led enterprise articles.

Days when I am not analyzing financial numbers, you can find me at a bookstore, attending Korean language classes or sipping matcha at a cafe.
